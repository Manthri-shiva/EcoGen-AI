# 🌍 EcoGen AI

## AI-Powered Sustainability Intelligence Platform

EcoGen AI is a next-generation sustainability intelligence platform that helps individuals, households, educational institutions, and communities understand, monitor, predict, and reduce their environmental impact using Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, Explainable AI, Predictive Analytics, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

Unlike traditional carbon calculators, EcoGen AI acts as a personalized sustainability coach by analyzing lifestyle habits, predicting future environmental impact, generating customized recommendations, simulating sustainability improvements, and tracking long-term progress.

---

# 🎯 Project Vision

To make sustainable living measurable, actionable, intelligent, and accessible through advanced AI technologies.

The platform aims to:

- Measure carbon footprint
- Track sustainability performance
- Predict future environmental impact
- Generate personalized recommendations
- Simulate lifestyle improvements
- Provide AI-powered sustainability guidance
- Encourage sustainable behavior
- Promote community-wide environmental impact

---

# ❓ Problem Statement

Many people want to live sustainably but lack awareness about:

- Carbon footprint
- Electricity consumption
- Water wastage
- Waste generation
- Transportation emissions
- Sustainable alternatives

Existing sustainability applications provide static calculations but fail to offer intelligent insights, predictions, simulations, and personalized action plans.

EcoGen AI addresses this gap through an integrated AI-powered sustainability ecosystem.
