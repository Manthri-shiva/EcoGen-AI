# Session 9 – EcoTwin AI (Digital Sustainability Twin)

## Objective

Create a digital twin of a user that simulates future sustainability outcomes based on current behavior.

The system predicts environmental impact before actions happen.

---

## Inputs

* Age
* Family Size
* Location
* Electricity Usage
* Water Usage
* Daily Travel Distance
* Waste Generated
* Sustainability Score

---

## Simulation Categories

### Energy

Predict future electricity consumption.

### Water

Predict future water consumption.

### Transportation

Predict future transport emissions.

### Waste

Predict future waste generation.

---

## Forecast Horizons

* 3 Months
* 6 Months
* 12 Months

---

## Outputs

### Current Footprint

Current carbon footprint.

### Future Footprint

Predicted footprint at:

* 3 months
* 6 months
* 12 months

### Sustainability Score Forecast

Predicted future score.

### Carbon Reduction Potential

Potential reduction if recommendations are followed.

### Cost Savings Forecast

Estimated annual savings.

---

## Integration

Use:

* Prediction Engine
* Location Intelligence Engine
* Sustainability Advisor
* Carbon Simulator

---

## UI

Create a dedicated EcoTwin AI page with:

* Digital Twin Overview
* Forecast Charts
* Sustainability Score Gauge
* Carbon Reduction Potential
* Recommended Future Actions

---

## Success Criteria

The user can visualize future sustainability outcomes before making lifestyle decisions.
